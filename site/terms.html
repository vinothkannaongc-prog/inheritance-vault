Technical tool, not a legal will

Will & Key is a software interface to an autonomous smart contract. It is not a lawyer, fiduciary, custodian, executor, tax adviser, investment adviser, or substitute for a legally valid estate plan. Laws and tax treatment vary. Obtain qualified advice for your jurisdiction.

Self-custody and immutable-contract risk

You control transaction signing and are responsible for wallet security, beneficiary addresses, asset compatibility, check-in timing, horizon settings, and gas. The deployed vault contract is non-upgradeable. A defect, compromised key, wrong address, unsupported token, network failure, or lost beneficiary key can cause permanent loss. The deployed contract accepts any token address, but only ETH and supported single-address tokens are covered by the published guarantees; with other tokens, value can be lost or become reachable by the administrator's surplus sweep. The contract has an internal adversarial review and a preliminary audit by AI auditing agents, performed at the project's request and not independent, but no completed independent third-party audit. Fixes from the preliminary audit cannot be applied to the deployed contract. Use only value you can afford to lose.

No alerts

Will & Key sends no alerts or reminders of any kind. Nobody will notify you if your timer expires or a claim is filed on your vault, and nobody notifies your beneficiary when a vault becomes claimable. Checking the vault is your responsibility and your beneficiary's.

Fees

The current global claim-settlement fee is 0.5%, capped at 1% by the deployed bytecode. Each vault's fee can never exceed the global rate in force when its creation transaction was mined. Lower global rates apply only while they are in force. When a claim starts, its rate is capped at the lower of the vault's ceiling and the global rate at that moment, and it can still fall before settlement. No fee is taken if no fee recipient is set when the claim settles. The administrator can change the global rate and the fee recipient without delay; the details are on the mechanism page. Blockchain gas fees are separate and go to the network, not Will & Key. Verify current contract state before signing.

Paid reminders are disabled

Will & Key is not accepting new paid reminder subscriptions through this website, and the reminder service is not running. The older reminder billing contract remains on Base, but sales on it were also switched off on chain on 26 September 2026: its price was set to the maximum, so every payment to it reverts, and only the administrator could reverse that (transaction 0xf3485b1b4ec0f887838a2eec5181c3815e68913bc23b68570c3b635693a56cca). A direct call to it is not an accepted service order. Do not send funds to it. The vault itself does not depend on the reminder service.

Existing reminder payments and refunds

The retired on-chain billing contract has no automatic refund function. If you previously paid for reminders and believe a service obligation or refund is outstanding, contact reminders@willandkey.com with the wallet address and transaction hash. Any refund or remedy will be handled off-chain where required by the applicable agreement or law; never send a seed phrase or private key.
