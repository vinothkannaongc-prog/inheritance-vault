Technical tool, not a legal will

Will & Key is a software interface to an autonomous smart contract. It is not a lawyer, fiduciary, custodian, executor, tax adviser, investment adviser, or substitute for a legally valid estate plan. Laws and tax treatment vary. Obtain qualified advice for your jurisdiction.

Self-custody and immutable-contract risk

You control transaction signing and are responsible for wallet security, beneficiary and payout addresses, check-in timing, horizon settings, and gas. The vault contract (version 2, deployed on Base on V2_DATE_TBD) is non-upgradeable. A defect, compromised key, wrong address, network failure, token-issuer action or lost beneficiary key can cause permanent loss. A vault can hold only ETH or one of the four listed tokens (USDC, WETH, cbBTC and EURC), and the list can never change. The issuers of USDC and EURC (Circle) and of cbBTC (Coinbase) can pause or blocklist their tokens, or change their code, which could freeze every vault in that token at once. Version 2 contains the contract fixes from a preliminary audit of version 1 by AI auditing agents, performed at the project's request and not independent; a few findings were kept by design and are listed as known limits. Version 2 itself has been reviewed only by AI agents and has no completed independent third-party audit. Use only value you can afford to lose.

No alerts

Will & Key sends no alerts or reminders of any kind. Nobody will notify you if your timer expires or a claim is filed on your vault, and nobody notifies your beneficiary when a vault becomes claimable. Checking the vault is your responsibility and your beneficiary's.

Fees

The current global claim-settlement fee is 0.5%, capped at 1% by the deployed bytecode. Each vault's fee can never exceed the global rate in force when its creation transaction was mined. The administrator can cut the global rate at once, but a raise takes effect only 30 days after it is announced on chain. When a claim starts, its rate is capped at the lower of the vault's ceiling and the global rate at that moment; a lower rate applies at settlement only if it is still in force then. No fee is taken if no fee recipient is in force when the claim settles, and a recipient set after a period with none takes effect only 30 days later. The details are on the mechanism page. Blockchain gas fees are separate and go to the network, not Will & Key. Verify current contract state before signing.
