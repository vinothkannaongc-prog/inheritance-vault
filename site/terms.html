Technical tool, not a legal will

Will & Key is a software interface to an autonomous smart contract. It is not a lawyer, fiduciary, custodian, executor, tax adviser, investment adviser, or substitute for a legally valid estate plan. Laws and tax treatment vary. Obtain qualified advice for your jurisdiction.

Self-custody and immutable-contract risk

You control transaction signing and are responsible for wallet security, beneficiary addresses, asset compatibility, check-in timing, horizon settings, and gas. The deployed vault contract is non-upgradeable. A defect, compromised key, wrong address, unsupported token, network failure, or lost beneficiary key can cause permanent loss. The contract has an internal adversarial review but no completed independent third-party audit. Use only value you can afford to lose.

Fees

The current vault interface discloses a 0.5% claim-settlement fee, capped at 1% by the deployed bytecode and by each vault's creation-time ceiling. Blockchain gas fees are separate and go to the network, not Will & Key. Verify current contract state before signing.

Paid reminders are disabled

Will & Key is not accepting new paid reminder subscriptions through this website. The older reminder billing contract remains immutable on Base and may still accept a direct contract call; such a call is not an accepted service order. Do not send funds to it. The vault itself does not depend on the reminder service.

Existing reminder payments and refunds

The retired on-chain billing contract has no automatic refund function. If you previously paid for reminders and believe a service obligation or refund is outstanding, contact reminders@willandkey.com with the wallet address and transaction hash. Any refund or remedy will be handled off-chain where required by the applicable agreement or law; never send a seed phrase or private key.

Availability, uninterrupted RPC access, accurate third-party token metadata, or fitness for a particular estate plan is not guaranteed. Nothing here excludes rights or liabilities that cannot legally be excluded.

The deployed contract addresses and source-verification links are published on the home page. The project repository includes source, tests, deployment records, and the internal audit report. An independent audit remains a release requirement for recommending use with material value.
