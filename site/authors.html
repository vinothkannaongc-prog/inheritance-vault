Authors and reviewers

This page states who is responsible for Will & Key content and, just as importantly, which independent credentials the content does not yet have.

Last reviewed 10 August 2026

Will & Key project team

The project team writes the current guides and maintains the website, Solidity contracts, tests, deployment records and browser interface. Its directly verifiable experience is with this project's implementation: designing the state machine, reproducing defects, running contract tests, deploying to Base, comparing deployed bytecode and testing watcher failure recovery.

Guides published under the project-team byline represent the project, not an independent view. No individual biography or credential is currently used to imply authority that has not been documented publicly.

Pages therefore avoid jurisdiction-specific conclusions and continue to state that readers should obtain qualified advice for their circumstances. When an outside reviewer completes work, this page will identify the person or firm, scope, date and any material limitation with their permission.

Standards applied to every guide

The editorial and sourcing policy governs primary sources, corrections, automation, commercial interests and review labels. Technical claims about the live system can be checked through the security page and public repository.
