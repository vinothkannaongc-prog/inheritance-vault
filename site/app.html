No owner address?

Search the vault contract's public records for vaults that have named your connected wallet as heir. Every record found is checked on chain, and only open vaults that name your wallet now are shown. The search reads every block since the v2 contract was deployed, so it can take several minutes, and it may be incomplete: a network connection can leave records out. Your wallet address is sent to your wallet's network connection and, if that cannot answer, to Base's public endpoint (mainnet.base.org).

Payouts owed to you

A withdrawal from a vault and a settled inheritance move nothing to a wallet: each is credited here, to the address it names, and that address withdraws it to itself, all at once or in parts. Credits exist only in the coin and the tokens the vault contract lists.
